# 短视频文案｜Good opportunities are never public

Good opportunities are never public.

The good jobs, they ask their own people first.

The good projects, they look for someone they trust.

The good deals, they move among people who already know each other.

Once it goes public, you are left with the leftovers.

Your real edge, is whether anyone thinks of you first, the moment an opportunity appears.

What is actually valuable, is not adding another friend.

It is a circle, where opportunities come to you.
